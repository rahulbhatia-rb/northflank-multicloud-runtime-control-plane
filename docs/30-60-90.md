# 30 / 60 / 90 Day Platform Plan

## First 30 days

- map cluster, account, network, and deployment topology across supported clouds
- baseline availability, deployment duration, recovery objectives, and infrastructure cost
- identify repeated manual platform operations and turn them into reusable modules
- document stateful workload classes and restore expectations

## Days 31-60

- standardize service onboarding and environment provisioning
- add consistent workload identity and namespace/resource boundaries
- introduce availability and topology defaults for production services
- codify observability requirements and service SLO ownership
- automate backup and restore verification for managed stateful workloads

## Days 61-90

- run multi-region and provider recovery exercises
- add stronger runtime-isolation tiers for higher-risk workloads
- introduce platform scorecards for reliability, recovery, delivery speed, and cost
- expand reusable infrastructure modules across AWS, GCP, and Azure
- measure developer lead time and reduce the amount of platform-specific knowledge required to ship safely
